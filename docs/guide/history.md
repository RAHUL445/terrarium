# Version history

In To-do or Notes, open an item and choose **History**. Select a version to
preview its fields. **This device** and **Another device** tell you where the
change came from. **Current** marks the newest version.

Choose **Restore this version**, then confirm with **Restore**. Terrarium
saves those values as a new change and syncs it. Each restore that changes
anything starts a separate version, labelled **Restored an earlier version**
or **Restored from trash**. The replaced values stay directly below it so you
can go back. Later edits can join that restore's version. Restoring from trash
brings the item back; restoring old values never moves a live item into trash.
Permanently removed items cannot be restored this way.

Edits by the same person on one device are grouped when no more than two
minutes pass between edits. A continuous editing session can be one version
even when it lasts longer. History is available only for tables the app has
chosen to expose, and shows their current fields rather than an old app schema.

## Keep history for

In **Settings → Spaces**, choose **Keep history for** beside your space:
**Forever**, **1 year**, **90 days** or **30 days**. This choice syncs.

Older versions are hidden and cannot be previewed or restored. They stay in
the space's sync data on your devices and can remain in backups. A longer
period can reveal them again. This setting does not erase old values.

**Current** stays listed even when older than the chosen period, but its
historical preview and restore are unavailable. Open the item normally to see
its live values. Restoring writes a normal edit; changes from devices that
were offline still merge when they reconnect.
